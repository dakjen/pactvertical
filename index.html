07 / Leadership

Dr. Gina Merritt, MBA, Principal

Dr. Gina Merritt is a real estate developer, social innovator, speaker, and community leader with 30+ years in real estate and more than $2B in deals. Since 2021 she has raised over $310M, and her development pipeline includes 800+ affordable units and 19 buildings across D.C., Maryland, and Ohio. She is Principal of PACT Vertical, LLC and of Northern Real Estate Urban Ventures, and founder of Project Community Capital®.

Raised in affordable housing in the Bronx, she built her career on Wall Street and in commercial real estate before focusing on development that connects residents to jobs, contracts, and entrepreneurship. She has managed development projects with individual budgets from $5M to $65M, from 21 to 347 units, and up to 350,000 square feet.

Before founding her firms, she held development roles at Neighborhood Development Company, Struever Brothers, Capital Vision Equities, and Clark Realty Capital. She holds an MBA from the Darden Graduate School of Business at the University of Virginia and a BBA from Howard University.
