Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

PACT Vertical, LLC ("PACT Vertical", "we", "us") operates pactvertical.com. This policy explains what information this website handles and how. The short version: the only information we collect is what you type into our contact form or send us by email, we use it to respond to you, and this site does not set tracking cookies.

Information we collect

Contact form. When you use the form on our Contact section, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, the service you are interested in, your message, and your phone number and organization if you provide them. We use this to respond to your inquiry and to evaluate your project. The form is delivered by Brevo, our email service provider, which sends the inquiry to our team and a confirmation copy to you. Brevo processes this data on our behalf under its own privacy policy.

Updates (optional). If you tick the "keep me updated" box, we add your name and email to our contact list in Brevo so we can send occasional news about PACT Vertical and our projects. Every such email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you at any time. We never add you to this list without that box being ticked.

Email, phone, and post. If you contact us directly, we receive whatever you choose to send and use it to respond to you.

Spam protection. The form uses a hidden field and a timing check to block automated submissions, and our server limits how many messages can be sent from one connection. No CAPTCHA or third-party tracking is involved.

Analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and understand which parts of the site are visited. It is cookieless. It records the page visited, the referring site, your country, and general device and browser type, and it generates an anonymized daily identifier from your request that cannot be used to recognize you across days or sites. We do not receive your IP address or any personal details from it.

Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address, request time, and user agent) for security and operations, as any web host does.

Cookies

This site does not set any tracking or advertising cookies. Third-party fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's request for the font files but does not set cookies through this site.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries you send us.
  • To keep the website working, secure, and useful.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers that run this website (Vercel for hosting, serverless functions, and analytics; Brevo for sending email; Google for fonts), and when required by law.

Retention

Inquiries and emails are kept as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any resulting engagement, and as our business records require. Contact-list entries are kept until you unsubscribe or ask to be removed.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Links to other sites

This site links to affiliate and third-party websites. Their privacy practices are their own, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
